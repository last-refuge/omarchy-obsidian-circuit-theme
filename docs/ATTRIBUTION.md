# Attribution

## Omarchy

The color palette in `colors.toml` is copied from the Omarchy **Starship** theme. Obsidian Circuit retains its semantic and terminal colors, including the `#ff9f0a` accent. The theme's own additions are the shell surface overrides, the wallpaper collection, and documentation.

Source: https://github.com/omacom/omarchy/tree/quattro/themes/starship

Omarchy's copyright and permission notice are preserved in the root `LICENSE`.

## Artwork

All four wallpapers were generated for this project with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. They are AI-generated artwork, rather than photographs or manually painted illustrations. They are included as WebP re-encodes at their native dimensions, without upscaling. Exact prompts are in `docs/artwork/`.

The source Starship theme's space-launch wallpapers are not redistributed here. The public preview is a new capture of an actual Omarchy session containing only theme demonstration content.
